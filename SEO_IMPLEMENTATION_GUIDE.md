#### 2. **Get Customer Reviews** (CRITICAL - #2 Priority)
Google heavily weights reviews for local rankings.

**Goal: 50+ Google reviews in 3 months**

**Strategy:**
1. **In-Person:**
   - Print QR codes linking to your Google review page
   - Place on tables, receipts, business cards
   - Train staff to ask happy customers for reviews

2. **Email:**
   - Send follow-up email 24 hours after visit/order
   - Include direct link to leave review
   - Keep it short and friendly

3. **SMS:** (if you collect phone numbers)
   - "Thanks for dining at East At West! We'd love your feedback: [link]"

4. **Incentive:** (optional, but effective)
   - Enter reviews into monthly drawing for free meal
   - Important: Don't pay for reviews or offer guaranteed rewards

**Template Email:**
```
Subject: How was your experience at East At West?

Hi [Name],

Thank you for choosing East At West! We hope you enjoyed your authentic Lebanese meal.

Your feedback helps us improve and helps others discover great Lebanese food in Brussels.

Would you take 2 minutes to share your experience?

[Leave a Google Review]

Thank you!
The East At West Team
```

**Responding to Reviews:**
- Respond to ALL reviews within 24 hours
- Thank positive reviews personally
- Address negative reviews professionally and offer to make it right
- Include keywords naturally (e.g., "Thank you for enjoying our Lebanese mezze!")

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Keyword Stuffing** - Use keywords naturally, don't force them
2. **Duplicate Content** - Every page/post should be unique
3. **Buying Links** - Google penalizes this; only get organic backlinks
4. **Ignoring Negative Reviews** - Always respond professionally
5. **Inconsistent NAP** - Same name/address/phone everywhere
6. **Slow Site** - Keep monitoring and optimizing speed
7. **Mobile Issues** - Test on real devices regularly
8. **No Local Content** - Always mention "Brussels" in content
9. **Neglecting Reviews** - Get new reviews monthly, not just once
10. **Impatience** - SEO takes 3-6 months; be consistent!
