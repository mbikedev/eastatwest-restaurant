## Guidelines for Creating High-Ranking Blog Posts

### Content Structure (Minimum 800-1500 words)

```markdown
# [Primary Keyword] - [Secondary Benefit/Hook]

**Meta Description (155-160 characters):**
[Compelling description with primary keyword + call to action]

**Focus Keyword:** [Main keyword to rank for]
**Secondary Keywords:** [2-3 related keywords]

## Introduction (100-150 words)
- Hook the reader with a question or compelling statement
- Include primary keyword in first paragraph
- Preview what they'll learn
- Add a personal touch or story

**Example:**
"Are you searching for authentic Lebanese restaurant Brussels experiences? At East At West, we've been serving traditional Lebanese cuisine for [X] years, and today we're sharing [topic]. Whether you're a foodie, a Brussels local, or just curious about Middle Eastern flavors, this guide will help you [benefit]."

[150-300 words of valuable content]

- Use H2s for main sections (3-5 per post)
- Include keywords naturally (don't force them)

- Add internal links to menu, reservations, other blog posts

## Keyword Research Process

1. **Use Google Keyword Planner** or Ubersuggest
2. **Target keywords with:**
   - Monthly searches: 100-1000
   - Keyword difficulty: Low-Medium
   - Search intent: Informational or Commercial

3. **Long-tail keywords work best:**
   - ✅ "authentic Lebanese restaurant Brussels city center"
   - ❌ "restaurant Brussels" (too competitive)

4. **Check "People Also Ask" on Google** for FAQ ideas

## Pro Tips for Maximum SEO Impact

1. **Update old posts regularly** - Google loves fresh content
2. **Add internal links from new posts to old posts** - Boost older content
3. **Monitor Google Search Console** - See which keywords are working
4. **Respond to comments** - Increases engagement signals
5. **Share on social media** - Drives traffic and social signals
6. **Build backlinks** - Share with food bloggers, get featured in Brussels guides
7. **Use schema markup** - Rich snippets in search results (auto-generated in our system)

Track these metrics for each blog post:
